RISE sits on the rooftop at Central Pattaya, looking out over the bay. Come for sunset with a saxophone and a live DJ, then stay for Japanese, Thai and international plates and three live performances every evening.

Book ahead and choose exactly where you sit: by the sea-view rail, on a lounge sofa, or close to the moon bar.
